About the Author

This is not self-help. This is recognition.

Marco J Olivier is a South African author whose writing begins with the questions people often carry quietly: Why do relationships break? What remains when someone leaves? How do we find meaning in loss, silence, faith, and change?

His work grew from a desire to understand human experience honestly, without reducing complicated emotions to simple answers. Whether he is writing about family estrangement, difficult relationships, identity, faith, or the search for meaning, Marco writes for readers who want to feel understood rather than instructed.

His nonfiction includes the Estrangement Series, The In-Law Series, The Art of Being Here, and The Shape of Series. Together, these books explore fractured relationships, emotional distance, presence, resilience, compassion, identity, and the deeper patterns that shape ordinary life.

After writing exclusively in nonfiction, Marco completed his first work of fiction, The Life of Death. The book is an allegorical story about Death, who has always carried endings, and Life, whose presence changes everything he believes about existence. Through their story, the novel explores love, mortality, transformation, sacrifice, and what it means to truly live.

Although The Life of Death marks a new direction in Marco’s writing, it remains connected to the questions that have always shaped his work. His fiction and nonfiction both look beyond easy explanations, searching instead for the quiet truths that people recognise within themselves.

Marco does not write as someone who has solved life. He writes as someone willing to look closely at it, to question what is commonly accepted, and to put words to experiences that are often difficult to explain.

Across all his work, his purpose remains the same: to create books that leave readers feeling less alone, more understood, and more aware of the meaning hidden inside their own lives.
