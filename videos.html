Videos by Author Marco J Olivier

On this page you can watch videos where author Marco J Olivier shares reflections and guidance about family estrangement, healing, and rebuilding identity after disconnection.

Family Estrangement Is More Common Than You Think

A reflection on how common family estrangement really is, and why so many parents suffer in silence.

Healing After Estrangement Starts With You

A video about beginning the healing process within yourself, even when reconciliation has not yet happened.

You Are Still a Parent Even After Estrangement

A reminder that estrangement does not erase your role, your love, or your identity as a parent.

Boundaries Are Healthy

Setting healthy boundaries is not rejection. It is a way to protect your emotional well-being while still allowing space for healing and respect.

Social Media Makes Estrangement Harder

In moments of estrangement it is easy to feel the urge to chase reconciliation. This reflection explores why centering yourself first can bring clarity, dignity, and emotional balance during painful periods of disconnection.

Estrangement Is Not Always Your Fault

Estrangement is often complex and rarely caused by one person alone. This reflection explores how parents can release unnecessary blame while still reflecting honestly on relationships.

Why Guilt Will Not Heal Family Estrangement

Guilt can easily become a heavy emotional burden during estrangement. This video explores why healing requires more than self-blame and how parents can begin rebuilding inner stability.

How Social Media Makes Family Estrangement Harder

Social media can intensify the pain of estrangement by exposing fragments of family life while real connection is missing. This reflection explores how these platforms can complicate emotional recovery.

The Power of Saying Less During Estrangement

During emotional conflict the instinct is often to explain more and argue harder. This video explores why restraint and thoughtful silence can sometimes protect dignity and create space for healing.

Why Holidays Are the Hardest During Estrangement

Holidays often magnify the emotional weight of estrangement. Traditions and memories can make absence feel sharper, and this reflection explores ways to navigate those moments with strength.
